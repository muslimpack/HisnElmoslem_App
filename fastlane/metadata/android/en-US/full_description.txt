Hisn Elmoslem is a comprehensive, ad-free, and open-source application that provides all the supplications (Azkar) from the famous book "Hisn al-Muslim".

Key Features:
- Ad-free experience with a clean, simple, and intuitive interface, in Arabic, English and French.
- Complete "Hisn al-Muslim" book content with search functionality.
- Custom Azkar: create your own titles and azkar, set repeat counts and reminders, and record your own voice for any zikr.
- Recitations: listen to bundled audio, or choose a reciter and download whole chapters for offline use or stream them online.
- Translation and transliteration of every zikr in English and French.
- Customization: Multiple color modes (Light, Dark, Black, Yellow) and font adjustments.
- Favorites: Easily add or remove Azkar from your favorites list.
- Reminders: Set alarms for specific Azkar, plus morning, evening and Monday/Thursday fasting reminders.
- Tasbih: Digital counter with voice-guided counting and auto-advance.
- Share Azkar as beautiful images, and back up or restore your data.
- Advanced Features: Control via volume buttons, background screen lock, and session restoration.

Privacy: no ads and no tracking. The internet is used only if you choose to stream or download recitations, and the microphone only when you record your own voice.
